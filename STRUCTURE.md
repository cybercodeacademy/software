## Color-Coded Categories

When organizing your files, think of each main category by its purpose:

- 🟦 **Blue (Operations)**: Products, Orders, Inventory, Shipping
- 🟩 **Green (Business)**: Financial, Legal, Reports
- 🟨 **Yellow (Growth)**: Marketing, Content, Platforms
- 🟪 **Purple (People)**: Customers, Team Management
- 🟧 **Orange (Support)**: Documentation, Tools, Backups

This color-coding can help you quickly identify file categories when organizing your physical or digital filing system.
